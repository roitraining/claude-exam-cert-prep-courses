# **Claude Certified Architect \- Professional:**  **Hands-On Exam Prep Workshop**

**2 days**

## **Course Overview**

This two-day workshop prepares experienced technical professionals to pass the Claude Certified Architect \- Professional (CCAR-P) exam. It systematically covers every exam domain to ensure you can confidently design, govern, and deliver enterprise-grade AI solutions on the Claude platform.

You will reinforce key concepts and sharpen your test-taking strategy through focused lectures, domain quizzes, four hands-on labs, and a final practice exam. Each module blends architectural theory with practical exercises and exam-style questions to build your confidence for exam day.

## **Learning Objectives**

* Prepare for the Claude Certified Architect \- Professional exam  
* Design end-to-end AI architectures and multi-agent systems  
* Apply prompt engineering, context optimization, and model selection strategies  
* Integrate RAG pipelines, MCP tools, and enterprise connection protocols  
* Evaluate system performance, accuracy, and cost-latency trade-offs  
* Implement governance, safety guardrails, and regulatory compliance controls  
* Communicate architectural trade-offs and manage project lifecycles  
* Enable development teams with AI-assisted workflows and operational tooling

## **Who Should Attend**

Mid- to senior-level technical professionals—including solution architects, AI/ML engineers, technical leads, and senior software engineers—who design, build, and deliver production-grade AI solutions using Claude.

## **Prerequisites**

While there are no mandatory prerequisites to sit the exam, attendees should have:

* 3+ years of experience in systems architecture or platform engineering  
* 6+ months of hands-on experience with Claude or comparable LLM systems in production  
* Experience delivering end-to-end systems from discovery to deployment
