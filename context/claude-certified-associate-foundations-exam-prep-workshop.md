# **Claude Certified Associate \- Foundations:**  **Hands-On Exam Prep Workshop**

**1 day**

## **Course Overview**

This one-day workshop prepares business and knowledge professionals to pass the Claude Certified Associate \- Foundations (CCAO-F) exam. It walks through all seven exam domains so you can apply Claude to complete business and productivity tasks with minimal guidance.

You will build exam readiness through focused lectures, short in-slide Do Now exercises completed directly in Claude, two multi-domain quizzes, and a final practice exam. Each module pairs conceptual review with a one- to two-minute hands-on demonstration of the skill being taught.

## **Learning Objectives**

* Prepare for the Claude Certified Associate \- Foundations exam  
* Structure, decompose, and iterate prompts across business task types  
* Evaluate Claude outputs for accuracy, hallucinations, bias, and audience fit  
* Select appropriate Claude features (Projects, Artifacts, research mode, chat) and models (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus)  
* Redesign business workflows and recognize when to escalate technical work to Claude Architects and Developers  
* Configure Claude Projects, system-level instructions, uploaded knowledge, and connectors  
* Apply data privacy, anonymization, and organizational AI governance standards  
* Diagnose and optimize underperforming prompts and workflows

## **Who Should Attend**

Professionals across operations, marketing, project management, HR, education, communications, business analysis, and consulting who use Claude as a productivity tool and build Claude Projects in their day-to-day work.

## **Prerequisites**

While there are no mandatory prerequisites to sit the exam, attendees should have:

* Regular, hands-on experience using Claude in a professional setting  
* A foundational understanding of structured problem-solving, workflow design, and digital tool usage  
* Access to Claude (claude.ai or Claude Desktop) to complete the in-class Do Now exercises  

No software development or API experience is required.
